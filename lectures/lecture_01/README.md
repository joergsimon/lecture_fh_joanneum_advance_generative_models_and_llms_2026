# Lecture 1: Generative and agentic systems

Lecture 1 motivates the course and establishes the systems vocabulary used later:

- model calls, workflows, and agents;
- explicit task-success and safety conditions;
- trace reading and earliest-divergence analysis;
- capability versus reliability;
- repeated-run evaluation, including `pass@k` and `pass^k`;
- course organisation, practical work, and assessment.

The reviewed slide handout and live interaction links are distributed through Moodle.
